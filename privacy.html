Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 14, 2026

LoveLock ("we", "us", "our") takes your privacy extremely seriously. We built this application with a security-first, "zero-knowledge" architecture designed specifically to ensure we never have access to your personal data.

1. End-to-End Encryption (Zero-Knowledge)

All photos, custom messages, Gallery Room media, room chats, private third chats, and unlock state are encrypted locally inside your web browser before they are transmitted anywhere. We use industry-standard Web Crypto API standards including AES-GCM for encryption and PBKDF2 for key derivation.

  • No secret keys transmitted: The cryptographic key required to unlock your vault or Gallery Room is generated locally. Role keys and room keys are carried in the link fragment and are not intentionally transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • No readable data: Our database only receives a random, mathematically scrambling sequence of characters (ciphertext).

2. Data Storage & Auto-Deletion

To provide short, shareable links, we temporarily store the encrypted, unreadable ciphertext on our cloud database (Supabase). For your ultimate privacy and safety:

  • Vaults and Gallery Rooms are designed to expire after the creator-selected window, currently 1, 12, or 24 hours.
  • Gallery Room updates, including room chat, private third chat, reveal state, relock state, burn state, and mock unlock requests, are saved by replacing the encrypted payload with new ciphertext.
  • Burn/revoke replaces the encrypted room state with a burned payload for future access, but it cannot erase screenshots, browser cache, downloaded files, or copies already made by recipients.
  • Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered, not even by us.

3. What personal information do we collect?

We do not require you to create an account, log in, provide an email address, or give us a name. The current accountless flow stores encrypted content plus limited public metadata needed to run the room, such as title, selected theme, role labels, scenario category, expiration window, media count, room status, and revision number.

4. Adult Media and Role Links

After Dark rooms are intended for consenting adults. Because the links themselves contain the secrets needed to join a role, anyone who receives a creator, partner, or third link may be able to access that role until the room expires or is burned. Treat role links like passwords and share them only with the intended adult recipients.

5. Mock Coins and Payment Readiness

Coin balances, unlock requests, and Pro plan cards are currently mock or payment-readiness features. We do not process card numbers, bank details, or live subscription payments in this version. If live payments are added later, payment details should be handled by a dedicated payment processor and covered by updated payment disclosures.

6. Cookies and Tracking

LoveLock does not use any third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels. We may use temporary browser session storage strictly for the mechanical functioning of the app (like keeping track of your current quiz progress).

7. Law Enforcement & Data Requests

Because of our strict end-to-end encryption architectural design, if we are compelled by a legal request to provide data associated with a vault, we have no technical ability to decrypt your photos or messages, nor can we identify who created the link.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
